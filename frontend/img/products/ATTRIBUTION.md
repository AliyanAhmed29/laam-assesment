# Image attribution

All imagery is openly licensed and was retrieved via the [Openverse](https://openverse.org) index by `backend/seed/fetch_images.py`.

These are **stand-ins**: real photographs of South Asian clothing, not photographs of the fictional products in this demo. LAAM's own catalogue images are copyrighted and are deliberately not used.
